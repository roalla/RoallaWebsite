# ROALLA Website v2 — Codex Implementation Plan

## Objective

Reposition ROALLA as a Business Enablement company that creates and improves digital assets that drive:

- visibility;
- revenue;
- customer value;
- operational efficiency;
- continuous growth.

Preserve the current homepage hero presentation and the current header's visual style. This project changes the story, information architecture, page hierarchy, service positioning and conversion flow—not the established ROALLA visual identity.

---

## Current-state observations

The existing homepage already has a strong foundation:

- The hero clearly references websites, custom apps, integrations, workflow automation and practical AI.
- The primary CTA is a service inquiry.
- The secondary CTA points to the portfolio.
- The header separates Digital Enablement from Programs.
- The site already includes portfolio proof, business enablement, insights, testimonials and clear contact paths.
- Applications are present, but they are positioned primarily as a technical service rather than as digital products that create revenue, customer value or operational capability.
- Visibility optimization is not currently established as a clear service lane.
- The homepage focuses heavily on what ROALLA builds, but the business outcomes can be clearer and more prominent.

Do not remove the current hero imagery, animation treatment, header layout, logo placement or premium visual style unless a specific defect is found.
